    4. Killing form nondegenerate (semisimple — Cartan criterion).
    5. R⁷ irreducible (commutant = scalars — Schur).

the only 14-dimensional simple Lie algebra (over C) is g₂.

Therefore Stab(Ω_W) has Lie algebra g₂(split).  ∎
